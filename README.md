# 🤝 CampusConnect

> Find the right person to learn, build, compete, and play with.

Studying alone gets boring sometimes. There are days when you want to practice DSA, prepare for an exam, work on a project, or participate in a hackathon — but you don't have anyone to do it with. 

**CampusConnect** is a student-focused platform designed to solve this problem. Instead of blindly messaging random WhatsApp groups asking *"Anyone interested in doing DSA together?"* or *"Need one more person for a hackathon,"* you can create a request and let CampusConnect match you with peers who share your exact goals, skills, availability, and interests.

---

## ✨ What is CampusConnect?

CampusConnect connects students across four major pillars:

*   🧠 **Learning:** DSA partner, Study partner, Lab partner, Exam preparation partner
*   💻 **Building:** Project partner, Hackathon teammate, Coding contest teammate, Developer/designer teammate
*   🏸 **Sports:** Badminton partner, Football teammate, Basketball teammate, Cricket partner, Running partner, Gym partner
*   🎨 **Other Activities:** Club activities, Competitions, Photography, Content creation, Music, and more

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Create your profile:** Tell the platform about your skills (e.g., C++, React, JavaScript), interests, and general availability.
2. **Create a request:** Specify what you're looking for (e.g., *Intermediate DSA Partner for Graphs & Trees, 7 PM – 9 PM*).
3. **Find smart matches:** The platform ranks potential partners using compatibility factors like activity, skill level, availability, and goals (complete with a match percentage score).
4. **Connect & Team Up:** Send a partner request. Once accepted, you can coordinate and start working together—whether it's a 1-on-1 study session or a full hackathon team!

## 💡 Why I Built This

College is supposed to be more than just attending classes, writing exams, and going home. There are so many things worth exploring—learning algorithms, building cool projects, competing in hackathons, playing sports, and meeting new people. 

The hardest part isn't finding something to do; **it's finding someone to do it with.** CampusConnect is an attempt to bridge that gap.
